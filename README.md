#### What I do with data

- **Data cleaning & validation:** turning hand-typed, inconsistent records into structured, checked datasets.
- **Reconciliation:** matching records across systems (e.g. bank transfers ↔ payments ↔ invoices) and flagging what doesn't match.
- **Reporting & dashboards:** KPIs for management and audits in Excel and Power BI, with the conclusion first.
- **Automation:** replacing repetitive spreadsheet work with scripts and forms that validate data at entry.
